11.1

Scope & Purpose

Document 10 §10.6 named this document as blocked on its cost figures — this is where those figures finally meet a revenue line. Every number below is a modeling assumption, clearly labeled as one, not a forecast to raise money against without real sales data behind it eventually replacing it.

Not financial advice

This is a planning model, not investment guidance — the assumptions are stated so they can be checked and replaced with real numbers as they come in, not treated as a promise.

11.6

The Bloom Internship as a Business Asset

Document 10 priced the Bloom Internship (§10.3) as a cost line. It's worth naming the other side of that ledger directly: an intern who rotates through all three operational roles and converts to Staff, per Document 8 §8.4's pathway, arrives already trained on this brand's specific systems — a real reduction in hiring and onboarding cost versus recruiting a Staff hire cold from the outside market. The stipend isn't only the compassion commitment Document 8 §8.2 describes; it's also this brand's cheapest reliable pipeline into trained Staff, and worth tracking as one in whatever hiring-cost metrics get built later.

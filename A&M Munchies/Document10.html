10.1

Scope & Purpose

Four explicit dependencies converge here: the Bloom Internship stipend Document 8 §8.4 deferred, the fire suppression pricing Document 12 §12.3 already quoted real ranges for, the licensing fees Document 9 verified, and the packaging/fabric/footwear vendors Document 7 left as open items. This document turns all of that into real Capex and Opex ranges, by tier.

Planning ranges, not quotes

Every figure here comes from current published market data, not a vendor conversation. Treat these as budgeting ranges to walk into a real quote with — not numbers to commit capital against directly.

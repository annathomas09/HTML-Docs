2.1

Scope & Purpose

Document 0 called this the "core offering" document — what actually gets served, and which part of it never changes no matter where a branch sits. This document finalizes the signature anchors §0.3 flagged as fixed-in-principle-but-undefined, then lays out how the fuller menu scales by format tier (§0.6) and by café-to-café-restaurant stage (§0.8).

2.2

Signature Anchors — Global, Fixed

Two items. Every branch, every format, every climate, no exceptions — per Document 0 §0.3.

The anchor product — and now literally the center of the logo mark (Document 1). Six varieties, sold as a set: chocolate chunk, walnut, caramel drizzle, nutella swirl, cheesecake swirl, and ganache.

2.4

Café → Café-Restaurant Menu Evolution

Maps directly onto Document 0 §0.8's three stages.

1

Café (current)

Bakery case, coffee program, light bites. Both signature anchors front and center.

2

Extended Café

Small plates added for early evening. Bakery case stays exactly as-is.

3

Café-Restaurant

Full dinner menu built around the Main Hall's kitchen. Signature anchors remain on the menu unchanged — see callout below.

Non-negotiable at every stage

Even at full café-restaurant scale, the Brownie Box and the Individual Boxed Desserts stay on the menu exactly as defined in §2.2. A restaurant wing doesn't get to quietly retire the thing the logo is built around.

2.5

Beverage Program

Coffee-forward, consistent with the "Workstation Coffee" and morning-coffee-run references from early in this project — specialty coffee is the anchor beverage category, with tea and non-coffee options as a secondary tier, not an afterthought.

Open — no signature drink yet

Unlike food, there's no fixed signature beverage the way the Brownie Box anchors food. Worth deciding whether one gets invented, or whether coffee stays a strong category without a single hero drink.

2.6

Packaging & Presentation Standards

  • Brownie Box: kraft or window box, sized for six — the icon-only mark (Document 1 §1.3) stamped on the lid.
  • Individual Boxed Desserts: clear acrylic, per the original reference — the mark stays off the clear panel itself and lives on a small paper wrap or base label instead, so the product stays fully visible.
  • Everything else inherits the mark placement and color rules from Document 1 §1.5–§1.7 directly; nothing new to define here.
2.7

Kitchen Sizing Reference

At Café and Extended Café stages, this is a bakery-production kitchen — ovens, proofing, cold storage, plating for boxed goods — not a full hot line. That changes at Café-Restaurant stage, where a proper expanded hot kitchen gets added alongside the bakery production it already has, sized against the Main Hall footprint set in Document 3. Worth treating as two distinct kitchen build-outs, not one kitchen that quietly grows.

2.8

Sourcing Philosophy

Global, non-negotiable: real butter and real chocolate in both signature anchors, everywhere, with no cost-driven substitution — this is the one place quality can't flex regardless of branch economics. Local and flexible: seasonal fruit sourcing for the boxed desserts, dairy origin, and coffee bean sourcing all adapt per branch. Full vendor-level detail belongs in Document 7, not here.

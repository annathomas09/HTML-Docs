0.1

Purpose & How to Read This Document

This document is the constitution the rest of the A&M Munchies suite is written against — the first branch's document set (starting with Document 9, Regulatory & Licensing Compliance, for the first embedded-tier location, within SCRIPTORIUM, Said-ul-Ajaib) and every branch set that follows it, in any city.

Two labels are used throughout:

Core — Fixed Everywhere

True at every branch, in every climate, at every format tier, with no exceptions. If a future document contradicts a Core statement here, the future document is what needs correcting.

Flexible — Adapts by Context

The principle is fixed; the execution is expected to change by branch, climate, or format. Branch-specific documents own the actual adapted values.

0.2

Brand Positioning

"A bakery-led café brand built around one repeatable sensory signature — warm light, living greenery, and water — expressed at whatever scale a location allows. Built to travel: from a full flagship to a single counter inside someone else's building, without losing the thing that makes it recognizably A&M Munchies. It starts as a café. It's built to become a café-restaurant wherever the market supports it, without ever needing to be redesigned from zero to get there."

Proposed wording — this is the one section in Document 0 that should carry your voice directly rather than mine. Treat it as a first draft to mark up, not a finished statement.

0.3

Core Visual DNA

Five elements. Four are locked. One is still an open gap — flagged honestly rather than invented.

01

Signature Light Form

A circular / ring-form fixture with greenery physically integrated into or trailing through it — present at every branch, in a scale-appropriate form. Chandelier-scale sculptural ring at flagship. A single pendant ring above the counter at kiosk scale. This is the one visual thread that repeated across nearly every piece of inspiration you've sent — it's the strongest, clearest signature candidate the brand has.

02

Light Temperature

Warm white only, roughly 2700–3000K. No branch, in any format or climate, uses cool or blue-white light anywhere a customer can see it — not in the kitchen pass, not in the signage, not in the washrooms.

03

Water Paired With Green

Wherever physically possible, greenery is paired with visible water. The principle is Core; the execution is Flexible. Flagship and Embedded Anchor formats can run a real water feature — Said-ul-Ajaib already satisfies this without building anything new, since the unit's glass wall faces straight onto SCRIPTORIUM's koi pond and granite waterfall. Kiosk format compresses this to a living green wall with a slim water channel or misting element.

04

Signature Menu Anchors

A small, fixed set of items appear on every branch's menu regardless of local adaptation — the bakery-box format is the strongest early candidate given what you've sourced so far. The exact items get finalized in Document 2; what's locked here is the principle that some items never disappear, anywhere.

05

Nomenclature & Logo Lockup — Open

No finished logo asset exists yet. This is the one piece of Core Visual DNA still outstanding, and it should be the first deliverable once we get to Document 1 (Brand Identity) — everything downstream (signage, packaging, uniforms, digital) is waiting on it.

Ownership & Revenue — Core

A&M Munchies is a fully independent business at every tier — its revenue, P&L, and ownership sit entirely outside SCRIPTORIUM, Thomas Aldenmere University, and the Equitas Global Education Board. At an Embedded Anchor location, SCRIPTORIUM's only relationship to A&M Munchies is as landlord: it earns lease revenue from the space, the same as it would from any other tenant, and nothing more. TAU and EGEB have no revenue relationship to A&M Munchies at all, at Said-ul-Ajaib or any future location. Exact lease terms are Document 9 and Document 10's to define, not this document's.

Said-ul-Ajaib is the working example throughout this framework because it's the only branch with a real site: floor-to-ceiling glass, north-facing, opening directly onto Scriptorium's backyard.

0.7

Climate & Regional Adaptation Guardrails

What a branch is allowed to change when it isn't Delhi, and what it never is.

Never Flexes

  • Signature ring-light form
  • Warm-light-only rule
  • Signature menu anchors
  • Logo / nomenclature

Always Adapts by Region

  • Indoor/outdoor seating ratio
  • Plant species (climate-appropriate, not a fixed species list)
  • Material weight & finish
  • HVAC and enclosure strategy
  • Seasonal / regional menu items
Worked Example — Said-ul-Ajaib

Delhi swings from summer heat well into the 40s°C, through a monsoon season, into a cold, fog-heavy winter with sharply worse air quality. A glass-forward, indoor/outdoor-blurred format that reads beautifully in a Mediterranean or tropical reference image needs real HVAC load planning for summer and an enclosed or seasonal fallback for winter here — it can't stay open-air year-round the way the same concept might elsewhere. This is a live input for Document 3 (Interior & Fit-Out), not something to settle in isolation at the brand-framework level.

0.8

Format Evolution: Café → Café-Restaurant

What changes when a branch scales up — and what doesn't.

1

Café (current)

Counter service, day hours, bakery-led menu.

2

Extended Café

Light table service added alongside the counter; hours extend into early evening.

3

Café-Restaurant

Full table service, expanded kitchen, dinner program, reservations.

Open — needs your input

What actually triggers a branch moving from one step to the next — footfall thresholds, lease renegotiation, local demand — is a business-planning call, not a design one. Flagging it here rather than inventing numbers; this belongs in Document 10 (Business Plan & Revenue Model) once you've got a view on it.

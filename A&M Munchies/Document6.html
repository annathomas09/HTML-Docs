6.1

Scope & Purpose

Document 0 §0.6 scoped this as "global philosophy + local policy." This document does the specific thing Document 3 §3.8 asked for by name — a real house rule for the water features — plus the wider code of conduct it belongs inside.

6.2

Service Philosophy

Document 8 §8.2 built "compassion for every being" as a staff and wildlife philosophy. This is where it extends to guests — the group it was always implicitly about. In practice: no minimum-spend pressure to occupy a seat during quiet hours, no rushing a table, and every rule below exists to protect something specific, not as a generic "because we said so."

6.3

House Rules — The Water Features

Document 3 §3.8 flagged this directly, after Baoding's Koi Pond Restaurant turned out to have real, documented incidents of guests throwing wrappers and tissues into their tank, forcing a public "edible food only" rule. Two systems here, two different exposure levels:

Right Extension — Garden Pond

  • No feeding the fish, no exceptions — not even "just this once"
  • No coins, no objects, no food waste of any kind
  • Open water, directly reachable — this is the higher-risk zone and the rule applies here hardest

Main Hall — Glass-Tank Pond

  • Sealed behind glass by construction (Document 3 §3.4) — direct contact risk is lower by design, not by rule alone
  • Family-size seating pods sit immediately around it — supervising children at the table edge is a house expectation, not a suggestion
6.4

Photography & Social Sharing

Encouraged, not merely tolerated. Document 5's entire content strategy assumes guests will photograph the food and the space, and Document 3's architecture was built with that in mind. The only real limits: no tripods or professional-grade shoots without asking ahead, and the ordinary courtesy of not putting a stranger's face in a shot without asking them first.

6.5

6.6

Said-ul-Ajaib — Specific to This Branch

Local — does not apply elsewhere

Said-ul-Ajaib, the first embedded-tier location within SCRIPTORIUM, gets a few policies a Flagship branch won't need. Embedded inside a study campus, this branch is laptop-friendly by design, with no aggressive seating-time limits during study hours. Document 5 §5.7's pre-order/pickup feature exists specifically so someone working in a Capsule pod doesn't have to break focus to queue. This is a Said-ul-Ajaib policy, not a brand-wide one — a Flagship branch with no adjacent study campus has no equivalent reason to adopt it.

6.7

Reservations & Café-Restaurant Stage

At Café and Extended Café stages (Document 0 §0.8), no reservation system — counter-first, walk-in by nature. Reservations become genuinely relevant at Café-Restaurant stage, most plausibly for the Back Extension's evening positioning specifically, given §6.5's zone read.

6.8

Accessibility

Flagged honestly rather than assumed solved: Document 3's stone walkways circling the central pond, and the Right Extension's loggia-and-pool layout, haven't been checked against wheelchair access or varied mobility needs. This needs a real accessibility pass against the Document 3 architecture before it's built, not an assumption that a beautiful space is automatically a usable one.
